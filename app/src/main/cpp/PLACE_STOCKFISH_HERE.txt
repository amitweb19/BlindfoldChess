i.e. copy the entire `src/` folder from the Stockfish-sf_18 download into
`app/src/main/cpp/stockfish/src/` exactly as-is. The CMakeLists.txt globs
all .cpp files automatically and excludes the upstream main.cpp.

Then place the two NNUE network files in app/src/main/assets/ :
  nn-c288c895ea92.nnue
  nn-37f18f62d772.nnue
These are downloaded by the upstream Makefile during a normal build; for
Android we ship them as assets and copy them to filesDir on first launch
(see NnueAssetInstaller.kt).
